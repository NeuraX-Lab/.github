# NeuraX

**NeuraX** is a multidisciplinary student team building data-driven and AI-powered solutions at the intersection of **space, Earth observation, geospatial data, environmental science, software engineering, hardware, and open science**.

## Current focus

### NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026

We are researching all 14 published challenges before locking our final direction. Our goal is to build a solution that combines rigorous use of NASA/open data with a practical user problem, measurable technical depth, and a strong interactive demonstration.

Current provisional shortlist:

1. **Field Shift: Adapting Farms with NASA Data**
2. **Earth Analog Locations for Moon/Mars missions**
3. **Harmonization of MODIS and VIIRS Hot Spots**

The shortlist is provisional until the complete 2026 challenge statements/resources are audited.
